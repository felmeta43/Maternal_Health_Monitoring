# Executive Summary

Maternal health remains a significant public health challenge in Ethiopia, particularly in
underserved areas such as the Gedeo Zone, where shortages of skilled professionals, limited
medical facilities, and weak routine monitoring systems contribute to preventable maternal and
neonatal complications. Continuous monitoring of vital physiological parameters during
pregnancy is often hindered by infrastructure and connectivity constraints.

To address these gaps, this project proposes the development of an IoT-enabled smart wearable
system designed for real-time maternal health monitoring in low-resource settings, supporting
remote consultation and timely clinical decision-making.

The main objective is to design, develop, and evaluate an IoT-based expert system that
continuously tracks maternal health indicators and provides automated alerts to healthcare
providers. The project includes:

- Integrating biomedical sensors into a wearable device.
- Developing a mobile and cloud-based platform for real-time data transmission and visualization.
- Implementing a primary diagnostic and alert mechanism (rule-based expert system).
- Assessing system performance, usability, and reliability through laboratory and field evaluations.
